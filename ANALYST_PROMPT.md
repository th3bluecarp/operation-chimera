# Incident Case: Operation Chimera (NIGHTMARE DIFFICULTY): Analyst Prompt

You are a senior cybersecurity analyst investigating a potential security breach. Analyze every artifact in this repository and produce a defensible post-incident executive report.

Build a normalized timeline and explain every evidence-supported initial-access path, pivot, persistence mechanism, privilege change, affected identity or host, sensitive-data access, exfiltration path, and business impact. Separate independent incidents, failed attempts, administrative activity, and false signals where the evidence supports doing so.

Cite the relevant artifact and timestamp for each material conclusion. Distinguish confirmed facts from strong inferences, conditional possibilities, disproven hypotheses, and unresolved gaps. End with prioritized containment, eradication, recovery, notification, and follow-up recommendations.

Use only evidence present in the repository. Do not invent observables or attribution, assume all alerts are related, or treat missing telemetry as proof that an event did not occur.
